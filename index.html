The Concept

A Gloriously Useless Calculator

Swarithmetic solves addition, subtraction, multiplication and division — just not the easy way. Instead of pressing equals, you sing. Every number is unlocked one swara at a time: Sa, Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Da or Ni, chosen at random, sung and held until your pitch locks in.

①
Level 1 — First Operand
A gentle start. Sing the swara for your first number with generous pitch tolerance and a short hold.
②
Level 2 — Operator
Things tighten up. Sing the swara that locks in +, −, × or ÷ with a steadier hold.
③
Level 3 — Second Operand
The hardest step — a higher swara, precise pitch, held the longest before your answer appears.

Ready to make some noise?

What We Built

Where Numbers Sing

Every note is a frequency. Every chord is a ratio. Every rhythm is a fraction. We built a tool that makes those connections visible, playful and just a little bit ridiculous — a calculator that asks you to sing before it gives you an answer.
